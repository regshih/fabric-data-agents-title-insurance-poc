# Deterministic title-order risk rules

| Condition | Points |
|---|---:|
| Unresolved encumbrance that requires clearance | 30 |
| Ownership or vesting mismatch | 20 |
| Instrument recorded after the search effective date | 20 |
| Target close within three days without a commitment | 15 |
| Open order beyond the 30-day service target | 10 |
| Repeated search failure | 5 |

Scores are capped at 100. Bands: Low 0-19, Medium 20-49, High 50-74, Critical 75-100. `IsAtRisk` means High or Critical.

The score prioritizes operational review. It is deterministic, excludes protected characteristics, and is not a legal conclusion or underwriting decision.
